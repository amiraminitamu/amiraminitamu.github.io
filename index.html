About

Academic profile

I am a PhD student in chemistry at Texas A&M University. My work focuses on theoretical and computational chemistry, especially electronic structure methods for molecules interacting with quantized electromagnetic fields.

Before beginning my PhD, I completed a BSc in Chemistry with a minor in Physics at Sharif University of Technology, where I worked on computational spectroscopy and quantum dynamics.

Research

Research interests

Theoretical chemistry Computational chemistry Cavity QED Electronic structure Many-body theory Polaritonic chemistry

My research centers on developing methods that describe how molecular electronic structure changes under strong light-matter coupling. A major current direction is the formulation of many-body second-order Green's function theory for polaritons, together with related approaches for correlated electron-photon systems.
